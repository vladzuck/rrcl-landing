01 · About us

A one-stop corporate partner in Hong Kong

Ross Royale Company Limited (RRCL) was established on 14 October 2020, at the height of the COVID-19 pandemic — a period that posed unprecedented challenges for international businesses. Many companies faced operational disruption, administrative hurdles and regulatory complexity, particularly in Hong Kong. Recognising this need, RRCL emerged as a trusted partner, offering tailored corporate solutions to support offshore businesses through uncertain times.

With deep expertise, an extensive professional network and a firm commitment to client success, RRCL has grown into a reliable “corporate handyman” — a one-stop service provider dedicated to seamless business operations for international companies in Hong Kong.

  • Who we are — a Hong Kong-based corporate services firm working with international and offshore clients.
  • What we do — formation, compliance, banking and business consulting, handled end to end.
  • How we work — a single point of contact, from first inquiry to filing.
